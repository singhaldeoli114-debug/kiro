The two of them

Aarav and Meera

Aarav is an architect who sketches every city he visits. Meera is a classical dancer who turns every room into a stage. They met over a shared plate of kachoris outside a bookshop in Jaipur, and have been arguing about the best one in India ever since.

“We found each other in the middle of an ordinary week, and nothing has been ordinary since.”

Our story

How this happened

  1. 01/March 2022

    Where it started

    A queue outside a bookshop in Jaipur, one plate of kachoris and a disagreement that has still not been settled.

  2. 02/October 2022

    The first long drive

    Nine hours to Bundi with a broken air conditioner. Somewhere near Kota they stopped pretending it was casual.

  3. 03/August 2024

    Meeting the families

    Two sets of parents, one dining table, and the discovery that both mothers make almost the same daal.

  4. 04/January 2026

    The question

    On a terrace in Udaipur at six in the morning, with a ring that had been hidden in a sketchbook for three weeks.

  5. 05/February 2027

    And now, this

    Back to the same lake, with everyone we love, to make it official.

Asked most often

Can I bring my children?

Yes, at every celebration. There is a supervised play area beside the Sangeet ballroom and again at the Reception.

Is there a plus-one?

Your invitation lists the number of seats held for you. If you would like to bring someone else, ask Priya — she can usually make it work.

What will the weather be like?

Udaipur in February is roughly 26°C by day and 11°C at night. Evening events are outdoors, so bring a shawl or jacket.

How do I get between the hotel and the events?

Shuttles run from the Lake View Residency lobby before and after every event. Departure times are posted at the hotel desk each morning.

Can I take photographs during the ceremony?

Please keep phones away during the pheras. The photographers will share everything afterwards, and the gallery here will be updated.
